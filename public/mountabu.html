DISCOVER MOUNT ABU

Experience the beauty of Mount Abu

Mount Abu is a beautiful hill station in Rajasthan, surrounded by green landscapes, peaceful hills, scenic viewpoints and a pleasant atmosphere.

From beautiful lakes and temples to peaceful natural surroundings, Mount Abu is a wonderful destination for families, friends and groups looking for a refreshing getaway.

Places to Explore

Nakki Lake
Guru Shikhar
Dilwara Temples
Sunset Point

Best Time to Visit

October to March is generally a comfortable period to explore Mount Abu and enjoy sightseeing.

Nature & Hills
