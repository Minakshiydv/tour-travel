DISCOVER JODHPUR

Experience the charm of Jodhpur

Jodhpur, famously known as the Blue City, is a beautiful destination filled with grand forts, royal architecture, colourful markets and traditional Rajasthani culture.

From the magnificent Mehrangarh Fort to the charming blue streets of the old city, Jodhpur offers a memorable blend of history, culture and beautiful views.

Places to Explore

Mehrangarh Fort
Jaswant Thada
Umaid Bhawan Palace
Old City Markets

Best Time to Visit
